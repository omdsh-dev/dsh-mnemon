Keep automatic spawn idle reviews hidden by default on DSH Hosts with activation-owned subagents. Turn off silent idle reviews to keep running them as visible children of the conversation and skip startup archival of verified orphan reviews.

适配 DSH 托管子 Agent 的空闲复盘，默认静默。关闭静默后，自动 Spawn 复盘仍会运行，会话会显示在当前对话的子 Agent 列表中，启动时也会跳过旧复盘会话的补归档。
